Privacy policy

R5 Ubuntu Backup

Purpose and data access

This private application is operated by its owner for their own encrypted backups and recovery. It processes the local files the owner includes in a backup and the Google Drive file contents and metadata needed to store, check and restore those backups.

Google access is limited to drive.file: files the application creates or specific files the owner explicitly makes available to it. Google data is used only for these backup and recovery functions.

Storage and protection

Restic encrypts backup contents on the owner's computer before rclone transfers them to the owner's Google Drive. Authorization tokens and recovery credentials are stored privately on the owner's computer. Restored files and any local working copies remain under the owner's control.

Sharing

The application does not sell backup data or Google account data, use them for advertising, or publish or share them with other people. Google processes the stored files to provide Google Drive. There is no separate application-operated cloud service that receives the backups.

Retention, revocation and deletion

The owner decides how long to retain backups and can delete them from their Google Drive. The owner can revoke the application's access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings and remove local authorization tokens. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not automatically erase existing backups or restored files. Those must be deleted separately by the owner.

These information pages

These static pages contain no forms, analytics, tracking scripts or application cookies. A website hosting provider may process ordinary connection information to serve the pages.

Privacy control

The application's operator and sole user are the same person. Privacy choices and deletion are managed directly by that owner through the local application and their Google Account.
